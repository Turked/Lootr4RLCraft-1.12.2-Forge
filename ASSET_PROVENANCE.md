# Asset provenance

The development JAR uses the installed Minecraft chest models, item sprites, sounds and menus through Forge and Minecraft APIs. The project's own indicators use geometry drawn in code. The project includes no extracted or copied textures, models, sounds, logos or third-party mod archives.

Before release, record any added resource's source, author, license and repository path here. Verify the final JAR against this inventory.

The PNGs under `qa/evidence/` are captures of disposable Minecraft sessions and a comparison assembled from those captures. They document third-party game and mod rendering alongside the project's overlay. They are outside all product resource source sets. Their inclusion as test evidence supplies no license to reuse the depicted game art as mod assets.
